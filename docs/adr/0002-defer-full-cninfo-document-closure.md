# Defer the full CNINFO document closure

The first research/development milestone will not run the locked 2011–2019 CNINFO body closure of 342,516 unique documents. The operator explicitly stopped future full-download activity after the v5 fail-closed truncation and the storage/latency review. Existing discovery and inventory generations, together with the v5 partial activity, remain immutable audit evidence and are not deleted or spliced into a future run.

The platform may continue its local development replay and the already implemented identity, market, CSI, and corporate-action seams. Any later production document acquisition must be a new content-addressed scope and Provider Acquisition Contract, likely restricted to an approved event category and PIT population; it must not mutate the current profile or silently replace its obligations.

## Consequences

- The three historical CNINFO transient units are stopped and no automatic full-document service remains active.
- Corporate-action, causal adjustment-vintage, and announcement-timed ST/suspension evidence remain blocked for the current profile.
- A smaller event-scoped acquisition can be evaluated without paying for unrelated bodies, but it needs its own exact coverage plan, source evidence, and independent Admission Verdict.
- Re-enabling any CNINFO body download requires explicit human authorization for the new scope; no old partial activity is an implicit resume token.
